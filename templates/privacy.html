Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. About VidsGrab

VidsGrab provides a web interface that lets visitors submit a video URL and request available video or audio formats. The service is provided without an account.

2. Information processed

When you use the downloader, the URL you submit is sent to our server so the service can retrieve video information and process your download request. While a request is active, the service temporarily keeps job status and the output file on the server. These job records are held in server memory and output files are stored in the app's downloads folder; they may be removed when the service restarts or its temporary storage is cleared.

Our hosting provider may collect technical information such as IP address, request time, browser information, and server logs for security, troubleshooting, and operation. We do not ask you to provide your name or create an account.

3. How information is used

  • To check a submitted URL and retrieve available media information.
  • To process, track, and provide the requested download.
  • To maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • To respond to messages sent to our contact email.

4. Third-party services

VidsGrab is hosted by Render. Submitted URLs are requested by the downloader software and the relevant source platform may receive normal network requests from our server. We do not control third-party privacy practices. If third-party advertising or analytics is added in the future, this policy will be updated to identify the relevant services and describe their data practices before they are enabled.

5. Cookies

The current downloader does not require an account or use cookies for a user profile. Hosting infrastructure or embedded third-party services, if introduced, may use technical cookies or similar technologies. Advertising partners may use cookies if ads are added; please review their own privacy notices.

6. Retention and security

Download job information is temporary and may be lost when the server restarts. We do not promise permanent storage of downloaded files. We use reasonable measures available to us to operate the service, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

VidsGrab is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices

You can stop using the service at any time. Do not submit URLs or content that you are not authorized to share or process. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at fayis510@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the website changes. The updated date at the top indicates the latest revision.
